How to Find Your Deal ID

How to find your deal ID

The CDA form asks for your MLS # and your dotloop Loop ID or zipForm Transaction ID. Both are optional. If you leave the ID blank, the office fills it in for you.

zipForm

Transaction ID (TID)

  1. Open the transaction.
  2. Look under the address at the top left. The number after TID is your Transaction ID.
  3. Your MLS # is in the Property Summary box.

In this example the Transaction ID is 123153887 and the MLS # is WVBE2051512.

dotloop

Loop ID

  1. Open the loop.
  2. Look at the web address at the top of your browser.
  3. The number at the end, after /loop/, is your Loop ID.
https://www.dotloop.com/my/loop/312105661

In this example the Loop ID is 312105661. Copying the whole web address into the CDA works too.

WAFFL: Where All Final Files Live

Put every final, fully signed file in the WAFFL folder in your loop or transaction. Compliance reviews only what is in WAFFL. If it's not in WAFFL, it doesn't count.
