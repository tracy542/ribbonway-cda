Financing Addenda

West Virginia · EPBR Conventional 01/2019, FHA 7/2019, VA 7/2019, USDA 7.2023 · 3 pages each · Buyer side, every financed offer

Attach ONE financing addendum, and it must match the loan type on the pre-approval and in ¶2C of the contract. Fill in the Financing Deadline, and on FHA and USDA put the sales price in the amendatory clause.

Conventional addendum FHA addendum VA addendum USDA addendum

Purpose of this form

  • Makes the contract contingent on the buyer getting the loan described in ¶2 (Price and Specified Financing) of the Residential Sales Contract.
  • Sets the Financing Deadline, how a low appraisal is handled, how seller subsidy is applied, and what happens if the lender requires repairs.
  • Each loan type has its own version. FHA, VA and USDA carry the federally required appraisal language, so no separate appraisal contingency goes with them.

Before you start

  • You have the buyer's pre-approval. Its loan type picks the addendum.
  • ¶2C of the RSC is checked for the same loan type, and the loan amount and terms are filled in there. Down payment + financing = sales price.
  • FHA: the buyer has signed the HUD "For Your Protection: Get a Home Inspection" notice (HUD form 92564-CN) before the contract.
  • Lender is Heather Adams / Revolution Mortgage? The PA Disclosure of Financial Interest is required.

Fill it in, top to bottom

All four forms — the same blanks

  • Top block — the date of this addendum, the date the contract was offered, every purchaser, every seller, and the property address. Match the RSC exactly.
  • Financing Contingency, ¶A — the number of Days after ratification for the Financing Deadline (¶2 on Conv/FHA/VA, ¶3 on USDA). Never leave it blank.
  • Initials — seller and purchaser at the bottom of pages 1 and 2.
  • Page 3 — every seller and every purchaser signs and dates.
  • The seller subsidy, lender-required repairs and deed of trust paragraphs have no blanks. The subsidy dollar amount comes from ¶2 of the RSC.

Conventional

  • ¶3Appraisal Contingency — check one: contract is contingent OR is not contingent on an appraisal.
  • Fill in the Days for the Appraisal Deadline. The form recommends a minimum of 21 days.
  • If neither box is checked, the form treats it as contingent with the Appraisal Deadline equal to the Financing Deadline. Don't let the form decide — check a box.

FHA

  • ¶3AFHA Amendatory Clause — the dollar amount is the purchase price from the contract.
  • ¶8FHA Required Notice — purchaser initials that they received HUD form 92564-CN.
  • No separate appraisal contingency. The form says so in its opening notice.

VA

  • ¶3AVA Notice of Value — no dollar blank. The form ties the contract to the value VA sets.
  • ¶1 notes the buyer pays the VA Funding Fee and may finance it. ¶5 has the seller paying any wood-destroying insect inspection required by the contract or lender. No blanks — just know what the buyer is agreeing to.
  • No separate appraisal contingency.

USDA

  • ¶5AUSDA Amendatory Clause — the dollar amount is the purchase price from the contract.
  • Note: the Financing Deadline is in ¶3 on this form, not ¶2.
  • ¶2 and ¶4 add the Rural Housing Guarantee Fee. No blanks.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • One financing addendum per contract. Never two. Cash deal: none.
  • Loan type matches the pre-approval and ¶2C of the RSC. Loan amount and terms are filled in.
  • FHA: HUD home inspection notice signed by the buyer before the contract.
  • Heather Adams / Revolution Mortgage: PA Disclosure of Financial Interest in the file.
  • Blank lines marked N/A.
  • Signing order: NOA → MLS sheet → dual agency (if ours) → HUD home inspection notice (FHA) → seller's disclosure → lead paint → RSC → WV ABA → Items to Convey → one financing addendum → other addenda → pre-approval or proof of funds.
  • Check the financing addendum box in ¶39 of the RSC.
  • Final, fully signed files go in the deal's WAFFL folder in zipForm/dotloop.

Common mistakes

  • Wrong addendum — FHA pre-approval with a Conventional addendum, or ¶2C says one thing and the addendum another.
  • Two financing addenda in the file.
  • Financing Deadline days left blank.
  • Conventional: neither appraisal box checked, or no Appraisal Deadline days.
  • FHA/USDA: amendatory clause dollar amount blank or different from the sales price.
  • FHA: buyer didn't initial ¶8, or the HUD inspection notice is missing or signed after the contract.
  • A separate appraisal contingency added to an FHA, VA or USDA deal.
  • Missing initials on page 1 or 2.
  • Revolution Mortgage loan with no PA Disclosure of Financial Interest.
