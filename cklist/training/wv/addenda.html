Contingency and Other Addenda

West Virginia · EPBR Home/Property Inspection and Radon Testing 8.2026 · Escalation 7.2025 · Septic Information 7.2024 · Buyer side, only when used

Only attach the addenda you're using. Every one gets listed in ¶39 of the contract, every "Days" blank gets a number, and every party signs.

Inspection and Radon addendum Escalation addendum Septic Information

Purpose of this form

  • Inspection and Radon — makes the contract contingent on the buyer's home inspection and radon test, with set windows to inspect, negotiate repairs, and void.
  • Escalation — automatically raises the buyer's price above a competing offer's net to the seller, up to a cap the buyer sets.
  • Septic Information — a short handout that explains the five common septic tests. The buyer signs that they received it. It has no deadlines or terms.

Before you start

  • The RSC is filled in. Each addendum's top block copies the contract's parties, property and offer date.
  • Inspections: ¶10 of the RSC is checked "contingent on inspections," not AS-IS.
  • Escalation: the buyer has told you the increment and the max price they'll pay, and whether they'll waive the appraisal gap.
  • Septic: ¶12 of the RSC shows septic. The Septic Information sheet goes in for the buyer to sign.

Fill it in, top to bottom

All three forms — the same blanks

  • Top block (Inspection and Escalation) — date of this addendum, date the contract was offered, every buyer, every seller, the property address. Match the RSC.
  • Signatures — every party signs and dates. Septic Information is signed under "Received By."

Home / Property Inspection and Radon Testing Contingency

  • ¶1aUtilities — Days after the seller says utilities are on that the inspection and radon deadlines extend to.
  • ¶2aHome Inspection Deadline — Days after ratification. Before it, the buyer delivers the full report plus either a repair Inspection Addendum or a Notice Voiding Contract.
  • ¶2bNegotiation Period — Days after the buyer delivers the Inspection Addendum.
  • ¶2cBuyer's Election Period — Days after negotiation ends for the buyer to void.
  • ¶3aRadon Testing Deadline — Days after ratification. Tester must be NRSB or NRPP certified and use EPA-approved methods.
  • ¶3bRetest extension — Days after the seller confirms the testing guidelines are in place, if a test has to be redone.
  • ¶3cRadon Negotiation Period — Days after the buyer delivers the Radon Inspection Addendum.
  • ¶3dRadon Buyer's Election Period — Days after radon negotiation ends for the buyer to void.
  • Initials — buyer and seller at the bottom of pages 1 and 2. Up to four signers each side on page 3.

Per ¶1b: if the buyer doesn't deliver the report and a removal or void notice before the deadline, the contingency expires and the contract stands. Watch the calendar.

Escalation Addendum

  • Increment — the dollar amount above any other offer's Net to the seller.
  • Maximum Sales Price — the buyer's cap. Never blank.
  • "Net" on this form = sales price minus seller concessions and seller-paid buyer brokerage compensation.
  • One selection required: Option #1 waives appraisal valuation (buyer covers any gap in cash) OR Option #2 keeps the contract's appraisal language.
  • Option #1 can't be used with VA, FHA or USDA. The form says so.
  • The Escalated Sales Price and Terms of Other Offer lines (offer date, price, concessions, seller-paid buyer compensation, seller's net, buyer's brokerage and agent) are filled in by the seller, with a redacted copy of the other offer. Leave them for the seller; don't mark them N/A.
  • Once escalated, buyer and seller initial the price change in ¶2 of the RSC.

Septic Information

  • No blanks except Received By — each buyer signs and dates.
  • It's information only. It does not create a septic contingency or set a deadline.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Only include the addenda you're using.
  • Each addendum checked off in ¶39 of the RSC. Escalation and septic go in "Other."
  • Every deadline filled in. Every other blank filled in or marked N/A.
  • Signed by every party.
  • Inspection: done before the deadline. The repair request or release is signed by both sides before the deadline.
  • Contingencies are removed or extended in writing before their deadlines.
  • Signing order: NOA → MLS sheet → dual agency (if ours) → HUD home inspection notice (FHA) → seller's disclosure → lead paint → RSC → WV ABA → Items to Convey → one financing addendum → other addenda → pre-approval or proof of funds.
  • Final, fully signed files go in the deal's WAFFL folder in zipForm/dotloop.

Common mistakes

  • A "Days" blank left empty on the inspection addendum — there are eight of them.
  • Inspection addendum attached but ¶10 of the RSC checked AS-IS.
  • Inspection report and repair request delivered after the Home Inspection Deadline.
  • Repair addendum signed by only one side before the deadline.
  • Escalation with no maximum price, or neither option checked.
  • Escalation Option #1 (waive appraisal) checked on a VA, FHA or USDA loan.
  • Price escalated but ¶2 of the RSC not initialed.
  • Treating the Septic Information sheet as a septic contingency.
  • An addendum in the package that isn't listed in ¶39, or listed but missing.
