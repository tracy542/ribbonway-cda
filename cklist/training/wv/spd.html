Seller's Property Disclosure

West Virginia · Voluntary Residential Property Disclosure Statement 7.2024 · Listing side at listing, buyer side every offer

Every listing gets one. The seller either answers every question and signs, or lines through the questions and signs the Disclaimer page. The buyer signs that they received it before they sign the contract.

Open the blank form

Purpose of this form

  • The seller's written answers, to the best of their knowledge, about the home's systems and condition.
  • The form says it is not a warranty and not a substitute for a home inspection.
  • It has two parts: the disclosure statement (questions 1–22, signed by seller and purchaser) and the Disclaimer (as-is, with any latent defects the seller knows about).

Before you start

  • Listing side: have the tax record handy for the address and legal description.
  • Decide with the seller up front: answer the questions, or line through them and complete the Disclaimer.
  • Other buildings on the property (detached garage, guest house)? The form says the seller may disclose those on a separate statement.
  • Buyer side: get the signed disclosure from the listing agent before you write the offer.

Fill it in, top to bottom

Page 1 — top of the form

  • Property Address and Legal Description. Pull from the tax record.
  • Seller — every owner's name.
  • Year built, how long they've owned it, and the dates they lived there. Never lived there? Say so.

Page 1 — property systems

  • Check every box that applies: water supply, sewage, heating, air conditioning, hot water.
  • Septic? Fill in the number of bedrooms it's approved for.
  • Fill in the age of the heating, A/C and hot water systems, and the hot water capacity.
  • Internet in the home: Yes or No, and the current provider.

Questions 1–22

  • Each question gets one box: Yes, No, Unknown or N/A.
  • Any "Yes" gets an explanation in Comments (or "If yes, please specify").
  • 3Roof — also fill in type of roof, age, and the fire-retardant plywood question.
  • 67Heating and A/C each have two questions: supplied to all finished rooms, and in operating condition. Answer both.
  • 9Septic — also the date it was last pumped, or check Unknown.
  • 10Water — three questions: water supply, home water treatment (check Leased if it is), and fire sprinkler, plus operating condition.
  • 12Insulation — walls, ceiling/attic, other areas, and where.
  • 13Drainage — standing water and gutters. Answer both.
  • 14Wood-destroying insects — infestation/damage, treatments or repairs, and warranties. Answer all three.
  • 15Hazardous materials — the list includes radon, lead-based paint, underground tanks and mining. If yes, specify.
  • 16Carbon monoxide alarm — applies if anything burns fossil fuel (heat, hot water, dryer).
  • 19Permits for improvements the seller or a contractor made.
  • 2021Flood zone / conservation / historic district, and HOA or deed restrictions. A "Yes" on 21 should match the HOA info in the MLS.
  • 22Any other material defects, including latent defects.

Page 3 — signatures

  • Seller — every seller signs and dates.
  • Purchaser — left blank at listing. The buyer signs and dates when they receive it with the offer.

Last page — Disclaimer

  • Used when the seller is selling without representations as to condition. The buyer takes it "as is," except for any latent defects listed.
  • Latent defects — the seller writes in any they actually know about. None? Write "None."
  • Every seller signs and dates.
  • Purchaser signs and dates that they received the Disclosure/Disclaimer.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Required on every listing, signed by the sellers.
  • Sellers may line through the disclosure questions and complete the Disclaimer page (as-is / latent defects) instead. If the questions are lined through, the Disclaimer must be completed.
  • Buyer side: it goes in the offer package before the contract, and the buyer signs or initials that they received it.
  • Signing order: NOA → MLS sheet → dual agency (if ours) → HUD home inspection notice (FHA) → seller's disclosure → lead paint → Residential Sales Contract → WV ABA → Items to Convey → one financing addendum → other addenda → pre-approval or proof of funds.
  • Every blank filled in or marked N/A.
  • Final fully signed copy goes in the deal's WAFFL folder in zipForm/dotloop.

Common mistakes

  • Questions lined through but the Disclaimer page left blank or unsigned.
  • A question with no box checked, or two boxes checked.
  • A "Yes" with no comment explaining it.
  • Not every seller signed.
  • Buyer never signed that they received it, or signed it after the contract.
  • System ages, septic bedroom count, or last-pumped date left blank.
  • Answers that contradict the MLS sheet (septic vs. public sewer, HOA, year built).
