Notice of Agency Relationship (NOA)

West Virginia · WVREC form, revised Oct 17, 2024 · 1 page · Buyer and seller side · Mandatory since 1/1/25

The NOA is signed first, before anything else in the file. A new one for every offer, the right box checked, and every client signs.

Open the blank form

Purpose of this form

  • Tells every party who you represent in this transaction: the seller, the buyer, or both.
  • Lists the duties you owe everyone, client or not. The client certifies they read it and got signed copies before signing any contract.
  • WVREC requires it on every WV transaction. It is how dual agency gets marked in WV.

Before you start

  • This is the first signature on the deal. Nothing in the file can be dated before it.
  • Have "A Guide To Agency Relationships" (WVREC, rev. Oct 24, 2024) ready. It's signed together with the NOA.
  • Know whether the other side is also a Blue Ribbon client. That decides which box you check.
  • Listing? Sign the NOA the same day as or before the listing agreement.

Fill it in, top to bottom

Page 1 — top half (the notice text)

  • Nothing to fill in. It's the duties list. Give the client time to read it.

Licensee and brokerage

  • Printed name of licensee — your full name as licensed.
  • Brokerage name — Weichert, Realtors – Blue Ribbon.

"Is acting as the agent of" — check ONE box

  • The Seller/Lessor — you're the listing agent; the buyer is with another company.
  • The Buyer/Lessee — you're the buyer's agent; the seller is with another company.
  • The Seller/Lessor as a Designated Dual Agent — both sides are Blue Ribbon, different agents, and you're the one assigned to the seller.
  • The Buyer/Lessee as Designated Dual Agent — both sides are Blue Ribbon, different agents, and you're the one assigned to the buyer.
  • Both the Seller/Lessor and Buyer/Lessee, as a Limited Dual Agent — you (or the firm) represent both sides and stay neutral.
  • The undersigned Seller/Lessor is unrepresented / Buyer/Lessee is unrepresented — used when the other party has no agent and signs this form. Unsure how to mark it? Email compliance before it's signed.

Dual agency — when both sides are ours

  • Check the designated or limited dual box that fits. Don't check plain "Seller" or "Buyer" on an in-house deal.
  • Designated: the seller's agent checks the seller designated box; the buyer's agent checks the buyer designated box.
  • Blue Ribbon also requires the Disclosure of Dual Agency in the file whenever both sides are ours.
  • The box you check has to match what the client agreed to in the dual agency selections on their buyer agency or listing agreement.

Certification — client signatures

  • Left column for sellers, right column for buyers. Room for three each.
  • Every client signs and dates. All sellers on the deed, all buyers on the offer.
  • Unused signature lines: mark N/A.

Licensee's signature

  • You sign and date. You're certifying you gave them a copy before they signed any contract.
  • Second licensee line: the other agent signs if there is one on the form; otherwise N/A.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Signed first. Nothing in the file is dated before the NOA.
  • A new NOA for every offer. Never reuse one from a past offer.
  • Signed together with "A Guide To Agency Relationships" (rev. Oct 24, 2024).
  • Both sides ours? Designated or limited dual box checked, plus the Disclosure of Dual Agency.
  • Listings: NOA dated the same day as or before the listing agreement. The listing can't be Active in Bright MLS before the NOA signed date.
  • Every blank filled in or marked N/A. Final signed copy goes in the deal's WAFFL folder.

Common mistakes

  • NOA dated after the contract, the listing agreement, or another form.
  • Reusing the NOA from the buyer's last offer.
  • No box checked, or the wrong one — plain "Buyer" on an in-house deal.
  • Dual agency box checked but no Disclosure of Dual Agency in the file.
  • Brokerage name or licensee name blank.
  • One spouse signed, the other didn't.
  • Licensee signature missing.
  • Guide To Agency Relationships not in the file.
  • Listing went Active in Bright before the NOA was signed.
