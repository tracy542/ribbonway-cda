Exclusive Right to Sell Listing Agreement

West Virginia · EPBR form 7.2025 · 12 pages (incl. Lock Box Addendum) · Listing side

Every owner on the deed signs. Price, dates and commission filled in, every "OR" choice checked, and seller initials on every page.

Open the blank form

Purpose of this form

  • Gives Blue Ribbon the exclusive right to sell the property for the agreement term.
  • Sets the list price, our compensation, any seller-paid buyer's broker compensation, and how the property is marketed in Bright MLS.
  • Collects the seller's representations: utilities, HOA/condo, liens, lead paint, FIRPTA, tenants.
  • Pages 11–12 are the Lock Box Addendum.

Before you start

  • NOA signed the same day as or before this agreement, with "A Guide To Agency Relationships."
  • Pull the deed and tax record. Every owner on the deed signs this agreement.
  • Have the seller's mortgage info, HOA info, and septic/utility details on hand for ¶8, ¶19 and ¶22.
  • Know the year built. Before 1978 means the lead paint disclosure.

Fill it in, top to bottom

Page 1 — the parties

  • Agreement date, Seller (every owner, as on the deed), Broker (Weichert, Realtors – Blue Ribbon).

¶2Property

  • Address, unit, city, zip, tax map/ID #. Lot, block, section, phase, deed book and page, subdivision, county. Pull from the tax record.
  • Parking, storage, historic district: fill in or N/A.

¶3Notices

  • Seller's mailing address, phones, email. Broker's office address, phone, email.

¶4Agreement term and listing term

  • A — the expiration date (11:59 p.m.).
  • B — the date the listing term starts "on or before." The form says the listing goes into MLS within 2 days of that, or as MLS rules require.

¶5Listing price

  • The list price. Seller initials page 1.

¶6Conveyances

  • B items that don't convey, C as-is items, E leased items. Fill in or N/A.
  • D — check: seller does OR does not authorize marketing the whole property as-is.

¶7–¶8Warranty and utilities

  • ¶7 — warranty cost cap and provider, or N/A.
  • ¶8 — check water, sewage, hot water, A/C, heat. Septic: # of bedrooms, system type, and is OR is not on a waiver.

¶9Broker duties

  • F — lockbox shall OR shall not; showing instructions.
  • G — sign shall OR shall not.

¶10Marketing / MLS / internet

  • A — MLS marketing authorized OR not (not = Office Exclusive; the MLS-required form gets signed with this agreement).
  • B — third-party websites. C — address display, comments/reviews, automated value estimate. One choice each.
  • D — Coming Soon yes or no, and the "Active in MLS on or before" date.

¶11Types of agency — two selections required

  • Limited Dual Agency: consents OR does not. Designated Agency: consents OR does not.

¶12Listing broker compensation

  • A — check one: %, flat $, or % + $. Fill in the tail-period days.
  • B — extra compensation if the buyer is unrepresented, or N/A.
  • C variable rate, D additional flat compensation at settlement, E retainer (will OR will not be subtracted), F early termination fee. Fill in or N/A.

¶13Seller-paid buyer's broker compensation

  • B — seller authorizes OR does not let us disclose that they'll negotiate paying the buyer's broker.

¶15–¶25Seller choices and representations

  • ¶15 disclose other offers: does OR does not.
  • ¶17 relocation program: yes or no; if yes, name, contact, terms.
  • ¶18 condo/co-op: is OR is not. ¶19 POA: is OR is not; dues, frequency, special assessment, name, management company, phone; current OR not current.
  • ¶20 property disclosure: has OR has not completed. ¶21 built before 1978: were OR were not; owner initials ¶21.B.
  • ¶22 liens: first and second mortgage lender, balance, phone (or N/A); check every box in C that applies.
  • ¶23 seller financing: does OR does not. ¶25 foreign person: is OR is not.

¶26–¶29Miscellaneous and additional terms

  • ¶26.A — licensed agent: is OR is not; underground tank knowledge: has OR has no; tenant-occupied: is OR is not.
  • ¶26.C — tenant lockbox authorization attached: is OR is not.
  • ¶27 farmland easement and ¶28 WV withholding: the seller acknowledges a farmland preservation easement and non-resident WV withholding. There's no box on the form; if either doesn't apply, email compliance on how to mark it.
  • ¶29 — any additional terms, or N/A.

Page 10 — signatures

  • Every seller signs and dates. Broker/Authorized Representative signs and dates.
  • Seller initials pages 1 through 9.

Pages 11–12 — Lock Box Addendum

  • Property address, date, and check Electronic, Manual or Both.
  • Sellers and broker sign. Tenant-occupied? Tenants sign too. Seller initials page 11.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Signed by every owner on the deed. List price, dates and commission filled in; not expired.
  • NOA dated the same day or before this agreement. Not Active in Bright before the NOA signed date.
  • Also at listing: Affiliated Business Arrangement (RESPA) disclosure, Equal Housing Opportunity form, Transaction Fee Disclosure ($299 additional flat commission, every listing), seller's property disclosure, lead paint if built before 1978, Bright MLS disclosures and seller options.
  • Start the CDA at cda.ribbonway.com within 24 hours of the listing agreement being signed.
  • MLS sheet goes in the file once the listing is live.
  • Every blank filled in or marked N/A. Final signed files go in the deal's WAFFL folder.

Common mistakes

  • An owner on the deed didn't sign.
  • Expiration date or "Active in MLS on or before" date blank.
  • ¶12.A: no box checked, or tail days blank.
  • An "OR" choice left blank — ¶6.D, ¶9.F/G, ¶10, ¶11, ¶13.B, ¶15, ¶18–¶25.
  • NOA dated after the listing agreement, or listing Active before the NOA.
  • ¶21 says before 1978 but no lead paint disclosure in the file.
  • Transaction Fee Disclosure missing.
  • A page missing seller initials.
  • CDA not started within 24 hours.
