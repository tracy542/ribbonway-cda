Exclusive Right to Represent Buyer Agreement

West Virginia · EPBR form 7.2025 · 3 pages · Buyer side, before you show or write

Get it signed by every buyer before you show a property or write an offer. Dates, compensation, and both dual agency choices filled in.

Open the blank form

Purpose of this form

  • Makes Blue Ribbon the buyer's exclusive agent for the term of the agreement.
  • Sets what the buyer pays us, and that anything the seller pays toward it is credited to the buyer.
  • Records whether the buyer agrees to limited dual agency and designated agency on in-house listings.

Before you start

  • The NOA and "A Guide To Agency Relationships" are signed. The form itself says the NOA is required and the Guide is included.
  • Ask the buyer if they've signed with another broker. ¶2 has them say they haven't.
  • Have the names of every buyer who'll sign, exactly as they'll appear on the offer.
  • Agree on the fee with the buyer before you fill in ¶6.

Fill it in, top to bottom

Page 1 — the parties

  • Agreement date — the day it's signed.
  • Purchaser — every buyer's full legal name.
  • Broker — Weichert, Realtors – Blue Ribbon.

¶1Term

  • Starts when signed. Fill in the Expiration Date.
  • Check it before every offer. Expired? Get a new agreement or an extension signed before you write.

¶4(f)Purpose

  • The type of property the buyer wants in WV (e.g., single-family home, land, multi-family). Don't leave it blank.

¶5Retainer fee

  • Dollar amount if one was collected. Otherwise N/A or $0.

¶6Compensation

  • Broker's Fee — fill in either the % of gross purchase price OR the flat dollar amount. Mark the unused one N/A.
  • Additional commission — a flat dollar amount the buyer pays at settlement no matter what the seller pays. Fill in or N/A.
  • Tail period (A) — the number of days after expiration that the fee is still earned. Fill in the number.
  • Whatever you put here has to line up with the seller-paid compensation you write into the offer.
  • Buyer initials the bottom of page 1.

¶7Types of agency — two selections required

  • Limited Dual Agency — check consents OR does not consent.
  • Designated Agency — check consents OR does not consent.
  • Both must be checked. "Does not consent" means you can't show them in-house listings that way.
  • Buyer initials the bottom of page 2.

Page 3 — signatures and contact block

  • Every buyer signs and dates.
  • Purchaser's address, email, phone.
  • Brokerage address and phone — your office.
  • Broker/Authorized Representative signs and dates.
  • Purchaser Agent Name — your name.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Signed by every buyer.
  • Signed before you show or write on a property.
  • Dates filled in and not expired on the day of the offer.
  • Compensation filled in.
  • Every blank filled in or marked N/A. Final signed copy goes in the deal's WAFFL folder.

Common mistakes

  • Signed after the first showing or after the offer.
  • Expiration date blank, or already passed when the offer is written.
  • Both % and $ filled in for the Broker's Fee, or neither.
  • Tail-period days left blank.
  • One or both ¶7 dual agency selections left unchecked.
  • Only one of two buyers signed.
  • Missing initials on page 1 or 2.
  • Broker/Authorized Representative line not signed.
