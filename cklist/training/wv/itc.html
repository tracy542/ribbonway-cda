Items to Convey / Final Walk-Through

West Virginia · EPBR Items to Convey 1.2025 · 1 page · Buyer side, every offer · Walk-through before settlement

Mark every item as conveying or not. Anything not listed does not convey. The bottom half is the final walk-through: done before settlement, signed by the buyer, with any problems written down.

Open the blank form

Purpose of this form

  • Top half: the list of personal property that conveys with the home, items the seller won't warrant, and anything extra. It's part of the offer package.
  • Bottom half: the Final Walk-Through Inspection. In WV this is where the walk-through is recorded. There is no separate walk-through form.

Before you start

  • Go through the MLS sheet and your showing notes with the buyer. What do they expect to stay?
  • Ask about anything mounted or leased (TV brackets, smart devices, water treatment, propane tank). Leased items are listed in ¶13 of the Residential Sales Contract.

Fill it in, top to bottom

Header

  • Seller — every owner's name.
  • Street Address, County, City, Zip — match the contract.

Item list

  • Each item has a Yes blank and a # (how many) blank.
  • Conveys: mark Yes and fill in the count. Doesn't convey: mark it so (No or N/A). No item left blank.
  • Garage opener: also fill in the number of remotes ("w/remote").
  • Heating stove: fill in the type.
  • Watch the look-alikes: refrigerator with vs. without ice maker; window blinds/shades/hardware vs. window treatments installed.

A. Smart Devices

  • List each one that conveys (doorbell, thermostat, locks, cameras), or N/A.

B. As-Is Items

  • Items or systems the seller will not warrant for condition or working order. Or N/A.

C. Other Items to Convey

  • Anything not on the printed list. Or N/A.

D. Items not listed do not convey

  • Nothing to fill in. It's why the list has to be complete.

Signatures (top half)

  • Every seller and every buyer signs and dates.

Final Walk-Through Inspection (bottom half)

  • Final Walk-Through Inspection made on — the date of the walk-through. Before settlement.
  • By signing, the buyer says the property matches the contract and items are in acceptable condition (the form points to ¶10 of the Residential Sales Contract), except what's written in Exceptions.
  • Exceptions — write down every problem found. No problems? Write "None."
  • If there are exceptions, fill in how they're handled: Seller to credit the Buyer $___, repairs paid from escrow per an escrow agreement, and/or Seller to correct within ___ days. Mark the ones that don't apply N/A.
  • Buyer signs and dates. The walk-through also has seller signature lines; get them when the seller is agreeing to a credit or repair.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Every item marked conveys or doesn't. Signed by buyers and sellers.
  • Walk-through done before settlement, signed by the buyer, with any problems written down.
  • Signing order: NOA → MLS sheet → dual agency (if ours) → HUD home inspection notice (FHA) → seller's disclosure → lead paint → Residential Sales Contract → WV ABA → Items to Convey → one financing addendum → other addenda → pre-approval or proof of funds.
  • Every blank filled in or marked N/A.
  • Final fully signed copy (with the completed walk-through) goes in the deal's WAFFL folder in zipForm/dotloop.

Common mistakes

  • Items left blank instead of marked conveys or doesn't.
  • Yes marked but no count (#) filled in.
  • Smart devices, mounted TVs or brackets assumed to convey but not written in.
  • A, B or C left blank instead of N/A.
  • Only one buyer or one seller signed.
  • Walk-through not done, not dated, or done after settlement.
  • Problems found at the walk-through but not written in Exceptions.
  • Credit or repair-days blank when there are exceptions.
  • Walk-through half never uploaded to WAFFL.
