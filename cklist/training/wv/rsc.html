Residential Sales Contract (RSC)

West Virginia · EPBR form 7.2025 · 10 pages · Buyer side, every offer

Fill in every blank or mark it N/A, check one box in every "OR" choice, and get initials on every page. Those three things are what compliance flags most.

Open the blank form

Before you start

  • A new NOA for this offer is signed by the buyer. Nothing in the offer is dated before it.
  • You have the current MLS sheet and the address and MLS # match.
  • The buyer has the seller's property disclosure. It goes in the package before the contract.
  • Built before 1978? Lead-based paint disclosure and EPA pamphlet go in too.
  • You have the buyer's pre-approval or proof of funds. The loan type on it is the loan type you'll check in ¶2C.

Fill it in, top to bottom

Page 1 — the parties

  • Contract Date — the day the buyer signs the offer.
  • Purchaser — every buyer's full legal name, the way they'll take title.
  • Seller — every owner, the way the names show on the deed (tax record).
  • Listing Company / Selling Company — the firm names. When it's us: Weichert, Realtors – Blue Ribbon.
  • Both sides ours? The Disclosure of Dual Agency is attached and the NOA box matches.

¶1Real property

  • Street address, city, zip, county, tax map/ID #, deed book and page. Pull them from the tax record, not just the MLS.
  • Lot, block, section, subdivision if they apply. N/A if they don't.

¶2Price and financing

  • Down payment + total financing must equal the sales price. Check the math.
  • Seller paid subsidy — the dollar amount of seller help toward closing costs, or N/A.
  • Seller payment toward buyer's brokerage compensation — fill in what the seller is paying, or N/A. Make sure it lines up with the buyer agency agreement.
  • ¶2C — check obtain or assume, fixed or adjustable, and the loan type. Then attach the one financing addendum that matches (Conventional, FHA, VA or USDA). Cash deal: check "not contingent on financing."
  • Seller and purchaser initial the bottom of the page.

¶3Time of the essence — selection required

  • Check one: time IS or IS NOT of the essence. Leaving both blank is a flag.

¶4Deposit (EMD)

  • Dollar amount, then either "in hand" or "received within __ days of ratification."
  • Check who holds it: Listing Broker, Selling Broker, or Attorney.
  • The deposit receipt (check or wire) goes in the file once it's made.

¶8Delivery

  • Delivery addresses for the seller and the purchaser, and both brokers' email addresses.
  • Check one: delivery to the broker shall or shall not count as delivery to the client.

¶9Settlement

  • Settlement Date and the Settlement Agent (title company or attorney). Don't leave the settlement agent blank.
  • The buyer has 10 days after ratification to contact the settlement agent.

¶10Property condition

  • Check the date condition is measured from: Contract Date, home inspection date, or other.
  • Check one: contingent on inspections (attach the inspection addendum) or AS-IS without inspections.

¶12Utilities

  • Check water, sewage, water heater, A/C and heat. Match the MLS sheet.
  • Septic? Fill in the # of bedrooms it's for and attach the septic addendum.

¶13Personal property and leased items

  • What conveys goes on the Items to Convey addendum.
  • List any leased items (propane tank, water treatment, security monitoring, solar). Leased items do NOT convey unless written in.

¶14Home warranty

  • Yes or No. If yes: who picks it, who pays, the dollar cap, and the provider.

¶18Purchaser's representations

  • Check whether the buyer will or will not live there as a principal residence. It has to match the loan.
  • Check whether we are or are not allowed to share the buyer's financial information.

¶38Expiration of offer

  • The date the seller must respond by.

¶39Forms attached

  • Check every form that's attached: NOA, lead paint, FHA home inspection notice, contingencies addendum, property disclosure, financing addendum, as-is, 1031.
  • Write the rest in "Other": Items to Convey, WV ABA, escalation, septic, and so on.
  • What's checked here must match what's actually in the package.

Page 10 — signatures and broker block

  • Every buyer and every seller signs and dates.
  • Date of Ratification — the date the last party signs. Fill it in once everyone has signed.
  • Broker block: company name and address, office #, MLS broker code and office ID, your name, license # and state, MLS ID, email.

Blue Ribbon rules

  • Initials on every page — seller and purchaser, pages 1 through 9.
  • Blank lines marked N/A.
  • Signing order: NOA → MLS sheet → dual agency (if ours) → HUD home inspection notice (FHA) → seller's disclosure → lead paint → RSC → WV ABA → Items to Convey → one financing addendum → other addenda → pre-approval or proof of funds.
  • Ratified? Upload the whole package as one combined PDF to the WAFFL folder.
  • Start the CDA at cda.ribbonway.com within 24 hours of ratification.

What compliance flags most

  • Down payment + financing doesn't add up to the sales price.
  • An "OR" choice left blank — ¶3 time of the essence, ¶8 delivery, ¶10 inspections vs. as-is.
  • Loan type in ¶2C doesn't match the financing addendum or the pre-approval.
  • Two financing addenda in the file, or none on a financed deal.
  • ¶39 doesn't match the forms actually attached.
  • Settlement agent blank.
  • A page missing initials.
  • No ratification date.
  • The NOA is from an old offer, or dated after the contract.
